FAQs

Helpful answers about content marketing and how it fits into a digital growth plan.

What makes content marketing effective?

Effective content is useful, relevant and aligned with the customer journey. It helps your audience understand your offer and trust your expertise.

Do I need content if I already have a website?

Yes. Websites need fresh, relevant content to support messaging, answer customer questions and reinforce authority over time.

Can content support my SEO goals?

When content is well-researched and aligned with user intent, it can help support search visibility and topical authority.

What types of content should I create?

It depends on your audience and business goals, but common examples include blog articles, service pages, landing page copy, FAQs and social content.

How often should content be published?

Consistency matters more than volume. A realistic publishing rhythm is usually more effective than irregular bursts of effort.
