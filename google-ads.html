Important PPC Metrics

Tracking the right metrics helps you understand what is working and where to improve.

CTR

Click-through rate shows how often impressions lead to clicks.

CPC

Cost per click shows the average paid cost for each ad click.

Conversion Rate

It measures how often users complete a meaningful action after clicking.

Cost Per Lead

It can help you compare spend against the cost of generating business enquiries.

FAQs

Common questions about Google Ads and pay-per-click strategy.

What is PPC?

PPC stands for pay-per-click, which means you pay for each click your ad receives. It is commonly used for search campaigns and targeted digital advertising.

How do I know if my campaign is efficient?

Efficiency is usually judged by how well the campaign generates clicks, enquiries or conversions relative to your spend and business objectives.

Why is keyword research so important?

It helps match your ads with real user intent and reduces wasted spend by targeting the terms most likely to generate relevant interest.

What is the role of landing page optimization?

Landing page optimization helps connect the ad message to the user journey and reduces friction, making it easier for visitors to act.

What does conversion tracking do?

It shows which campaigns, keywords or ad variations are creating meaningful actions such as calls, leads and inquiry forms.
