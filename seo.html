SEO FAQs

Answers to common questions about search optimization and strategy.

What is SEO and why does it matter?

SEO is the process of improving how your website appears in organic search results. It matters because users searching for your services are often already interested in what you offer.

How is SEO different from paid ads?

SEO supports long-term visibility through search relevance and website quality, while paid ads create faster traffic but usually require ongoing spend to maintain visibility.

Do I need SEO if I already run Google Ads?

Yes. Paid search can capture immediate demand, while SEO helps support long-term visibility and a more complete acquisition mix.

What is technical SEO?

Technical SEO covers site crawlability, speed, mobile usability, indexation and page structure, all of which influence how well your site performs in search.

How often should SEO be reviewed?

SEO should be reviewed regularly because search trends, competitor movement and site performance change over time. Ongoing refinement is part of a healthy strategy.
