FAQs

Common questions about social media marketing and how it supports business growth.

How do I know which social media platforms are right for my business?

The right platform depends on where your audience spends time and what kinds of content support your business activity. We review these factors before recommending a strategy.

Is social media marketing only about posting content?

No. It includes content planning, audience engagement, brand positioning, campaign coordination and performance review.

Can social media support my website and sales goals?

Yes. Social media can strengthen brand trust, bring more people to your website and support lead generation or conversion intent when the strategy is aligned with your goals.

Do I need paid social ads?

Not always. Organic content can be highly effective, while paid ads can be useful for expanding reach or supporting specific campaigns.

How often should content be posted?

Consistency matters more than volume. A realistic cadence that your team can maintain tends to be more effective than irregular content bursts.
